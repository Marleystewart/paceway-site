About
Earn Your Day is more than a run club — it's a movement built on community, culture, and showing up for yourself. Earned. Never given. Every Saturday at 9AM we meet at Spring Lake Park in South Plainfield to run, sweat, and build together. Beyond the miles, EYD brings the culture with day parties, socials, and events that bring people together. Music. Food. Community.
