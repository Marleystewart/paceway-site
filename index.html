Our Story

Built By Athletes
Who Live It.

Paceway was co-founded by Marley Stewart and Treymayne Russell while attending Trinity College in Hartford, Connecticut. As athletes who both share a passion for running and community, we saw the same problem everywhere: finding local run clubs was harder than it should be. Clubs were stuck on Instagram and word of mouth. Runners showed up not knowing the vibe, the pace, or anyone there. So we built the platform run club culture has been waiting for.

Built for club organizers too.
Stop managing RSVPs in group chats and Instagram DMs. Paceway gives your club a real home — and connects you with runners actively looking for a club like yours.
  • Your own club page with schedule, pace, and vibe
  • Manage RSVPs and see who's coming to every run
  • Reach runners in your neighborhood who are searching right now
  • Founding partner access — limited spots available
